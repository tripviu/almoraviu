## Day 1: Arrival & Exploring Sultanahmet  
Welcome to Istanbul! Begin your trip with a visit to the **Blue Mosque** and **Hagia Sophia**.  
Enjoy dinner at a certified halal restaurant like *Saray Muhallebicisi*.  

## Day 2: Bosphorus Cruise & Grand Bazaar  
Take a morning cruise to enjoy breathtaking views. Shop for souvenirs in the Grand Bazaar.  

> **Tip:** Most restaurants in tourist areas have halal options, but always confirm.  

## Day 3: Farewell  
Relax at a hammam before your flight home.
